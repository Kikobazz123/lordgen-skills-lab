# Pitch: Solo GP / Family Practice - Prospective client

## Opening
A patient calls back for a follow-up. The notes from three visits ago are somewhere in the system, but finding them means stopping to search instead of picking up where the conversation left off.

## Situation
The practice runs a separate consulting office from the exam room. Visit notes, test results, and history accumulate across both, entered at different points, and finding a specific past detail means searching for it rather than already knowing it.

## Complication
That search costs time in the middle of a consultation, right when a patient is sitting across the desk waiting. And what doesn't get searched doesn't get used, so patterns across visits are easy to miss.

## Recommendation: AI Knowledge Assistant
A system that surfaces a patient's past visit notes and test results the moment their file is opened, so context is already there instead of hunted for. Not a full records overhaul. Just faster access to what's already recorded.

## Proof
Early engagement. No case study yet for this specific offer.
