# Pitch: Facial Beautification & Skincare Shop Owner - Prospective client

## Opening
A facial was booked for 2pm. At 2:05 the chair is still empty, and it's the first thing anyone sees on walking in, because the treatment area sits right at the entrance.

## Situation
The shop is one open room. Walk through the door and the treatment section is immediately visible, with no separate waiting area to absorb a no-show. Skincare products (creams, serums) sit out on a table near that same space, with the rest of the stock kept in a cabinet. Bookings get made, but nothing between the booking and the appointment closes the gap for a client who forgets or changes their mind.

## Complication
An empty chair at 2pm isn't private. It's the first thing a walk-in customer sees, and it's a treatment slot that's gone for the day. On the product side, there's no record of which client bought which cream or when they're likely running low, so a natural moment to reach out passes unnoticed.

## Recommendation: Sales/CRM Automation
One system doing two things: automated booking reminders and confirmations that close the gap between "booked" and "shows up," and a simple record of what each client has bought so a restock nudge goes out when they're actually due for more. Not a general CRM. Just those two.

## Proof
Early engagement. No case study yet for this specific offer.

## The Ask
Let's spend 20 minutes walking through how bookings and product sales get tracked today, before we scope anything further.
